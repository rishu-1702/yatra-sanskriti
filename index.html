Raghurajpur Pattachitra Heritage Village

Living Culture & History:

India's first declared Heritage Crafts Village where every home is an active artisan studio. Preserves Pattachitra (5th century BC cloth-scroll painting), Tala Pattachitra (palm leaf engraving), and cow-dung papier-mâché toys. Birthplace of Odissi legend Guru Kelucharan Mohapatra.

Konark Sun Temple (The Black Pagoda)

History & Architectural Marvel:

Built in 1250 CE by King Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga Dynasty. Designed as a colossal 24-wheeled chariot of Surya pulled by 7 galloping stone steeds. The wheels function as precise sundials calculating time to the exact minute.

Ratnagiri & Lalitgiri Buddhist Viharas

Living Culture & History:

A premier Buddhist academic seat between the 5th and 12th centuries CE rivaling Nalanda. Celebrated for its exquisitely carved green chlorite stone portal, giant monolithic Buddha heads, and rare Tibetan-style Vajrayana tantric iconography.
